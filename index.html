Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MyoCoach.

How does MyoCoach calculate muscle fatigue? +
MyoCoach tracks the target muscle groups trained in each exercise, factoring in total volume (sets x reps x weight) and elapsed recovery time to estimate readiness dynamically.
Is MyoCoach free to use? +
Yes! You can download MyoCoach from the Google Play Store and start logging workouts and viewing muscle recovery immediately.
Can I create custom exercises and routines? +
Yes. You can build personalized workout routines, target specific muscle groups, and customize exercise selections to match your gym equipment.
Is my fitness data kept private? +
Yes. We take privacy seriously. We never sell your personal or workout data to third parties. Review our complete Privacy Policy for full details.
