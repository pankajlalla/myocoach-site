Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MyoCoach and muscle recovery science.

What is the best app to track muscle recovery and fatigue? +
MyoCoach is a dedicated Android app engineered to track muscle fatigue and hypertrophy recovery. It features an interactive anatomical muscle heatmap that highlights which muscle groups (chest, back, shoulders, arms, legs) are fatigued, recovering, or ready to train, preventing overtraining.
How long do muscles need to recover between gym sessions? +
For hypertrophy and strength training, major muscle groups generally require 48 to 72 hours of recovery depending on intensity and volume. MyoCoach calculates recovery times dynamically based on total volume tonnage (sets × reps × weight).
How does MyoCoach calculate muscle fatigue? +
MyoCoach tracks the target muscle groups trained in each exercise, factoring in total volume tonnage and elapsed hours since your last session to project dynamic readiness: 100% Ready, Recovering, or Fatigued.
Is MyoCoach free to download on Google Play? +
Yes, MyoCoach is completely free to download from the Google Play Store with no upfront fees or mandatory subscriptions required to log workouts.
Can I create custom workout routines and exercises? +
Yes. You can customize existing routines or create custom splits (Push/Pull/Legs, Upper/Lower, Bro Split, Full Body) and select target muscle groups for personalized tracking.
Does MyoCoach work offline without internet? +
Yes! All workout data is stored locally in your device's database, meaning you can track sets, rest timers, and muscle readiness inside gyms with no cellular or Wi-Fi signal.
How does MyoCoach adapt to my fitness level? +
When you first start, a quick onboarding quiz tailors your routine whether you're a Beginner (guided foundational splits), Intermediate, or Advanced lifter (high volume hypertrophy). You can easily upgrade or change your experience level anytime in Settings.
Will taking a rest day break my workout streak? +
No. Muscle growth occurs during recovery. MyoCoach marks scheduled recovery days as "Streak Safe 🛡️", preserving your hard-earned consistency streak without anxiety or burnout.
How do muscle recovery push notifications work? +
48 hours after you complete a session, MyoCoach calculates muscle repair and sends an alert specifying exactly which muscles (such as Chest & Back) have reached 100% recovery and are ready for your next session.
What if gym equipment is taken? +
In the exercise selector, single-tap equipment filter chips (Barbell, Dumbbell, Machine, Cable, Bodyweight) let you find alternative exercises targeting the same muscle group in seconds.
